# Is the iPhone 11 still supported in 2026?

**Short answer: yes, fully.** On 18 October 2026 the **iPhone 11, iPhone 11 Pro and iPhone 11 Pro Max** all run **iOS 27**, Apple's current version, and got **iOS 27.0.1 on 28 September 2026**. Apple surprised everyone by not dropping a single iPhone this year. The catch: the iPhone 11 is now the **oldest iPhone on the iOS 27 list**, so it's the most likely to miss **iOS 28** in 2027. Even then, Apple's track record says security fixes would keep coming for a good while after that. Apple doesn't publish end dates, so those last two points are **our estimate**.

## The facts (checked 18 October 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| Announced | 10 September 2019, in stores from 20 September 2019, with iOS 13 and the A13 Bionic chip |
| Major iOS versions it got | iOS 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 26 and 27 (seven upgrades; Apple jumped from 18 to 26 in 2025) |
| Current version | iOS 27 (14 Sep 2026), latest iOS 27.0.1 (28 Sep 2026) |
| Also offered | iOS 26.7.1 (28 Sep 2026), if you prefer to stay on iOS 26 for now |
| Apple Intelligence / Siri AI | Not supported (needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer) |
| Official end-of-support date | None published by Apple |

Apple's [How to download iOS 27](https://support.apple.com/en-us/128058) page lists the **iPhone 11, iPhone 11 Pro and iPhone 11 Pro Max** and the **iPhone SE (2nd generation and later)** at the bottom of the compatible list. Apple's [security releases page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100) shows **iOS 27** (14 September 2026) and **iOS 27.0.1** (28 September 2026) for "iPhone 11 and later", and also **iOS 26.7.1** on the same day for "iPhone 11 and later". The iOS 27.1 build released on 12 October 2026 is only for the new iPhone Duo foldable, not for other iPhones ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/iphone-duo-day-one-update/)).

## Why didn't iOS 27 drop the iPhone 11?

Apple didn't say. When it announced iOS 27 in June 2026, it simply kept the same requirements as iOS 26 and focused on performance, including a CPU scheduler that was ported back to older iPhones ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/06/ios-27-and-ipados-27-dont-drop-support-for-any-iphones-and-just-a-few-ipads/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2189977/ios-27-can-run-on-phones-as-old-as-the-iphone-11/)). iPadOS 27 did drop some iPads with the older A12 chip, while iPhones with the A13 (the iPhone 11 family and the iPhone SE 2nd generation) all made the cut.

What the iPhone 11 **doesn't** get is the AI layer. Apple Intelligence and the new Siri AI need an **iPhone 15 Pro or newer**, so an iPhone 11 on iOS 27 gets the core update and security fixes, but not those features ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/phones/ios/ios-27-and-siri-ai-compatibility-explained)).

## When will the iPhone 11 stop getting updates?

Apple doesn't announce it, so nobody can give you an exact date. Here's what Apple's own history suggests:

- **Major versions:** the iPhone XS and XR (2018) were dropped by iOS 26 in 2025, after about seven years. The iPhone 11 (2019) has already reached seven years with iOS 27. **Our estimate:** iOS 28 in September 2027 is the first version that could leave it out. Apple hasn't said anything about iOS 28 yet.
- **Security fixes after that:** Apple keeps patching dropped iPhones on their last iOS line. The **iPhone 8 and iPhone X** lost new versions with iOS 17 in September 2023 and were still getting **iOS 16.7.16 on 11 May 2026** ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100)). The XR, dropped in 2025, got **iOS 18.7.10 in August 2026**. See [Is the iPhone XR still supported?](../is-iphone-xr-still-supported/) and [Is the iPhone X still supported?](../is-iphone-x-still-supported/)
- **Our estimate:** full support **at least until September 2027**, then security-only updates on iOS 27, probably **into 2029**. That's our reading of the track record, not a promise from Apple.

## Should you keep using an iPhone 11?

1. **Install iOS 27.0.1 now** (or iOS 26.7.1 if you're staying on iOS 26): Settings → General → Software Update.
2. **Keep using it with confidence for now.** It's on the current iOS, so banking, payments, DigiD, WhatsApp and work email are fine.
3. **Watch WWDC in June 2027.** That's when Apple will reveal whether iOS 28 still supports it. If it doesn't, you'll still have security fixes for a while, so there's no need to panic-buy.
4. **Check the battery.** On a 2019 phone, the battery is usually the first thing to go. Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging shows its maximum capacity.

## Is a used iPhone 11 still worth buying?

Only if it's cheap and you're happy with "a couple of years". You get iOS 27 today, but it's the iPhone most likely to miss the next version. For a little more, an **iPhone 13** or **iPhone 15** has more years left on the same iOS list. If you're buying one for a child or a parent who just needs WhatsApp and photos, a cheap iPhone 11 with a healthy battery is fine for now.
