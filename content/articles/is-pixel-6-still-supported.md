# Is the Pixel 6 still supported?

**Short answer: this is the last month.** Google guarantees the Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro updates **until October 2026**. Both phones got the **October 2026 security update** (patch level 2026-10-05) on 6 October 2026, and Google has made no promise of anything after that. Unless Google extends support again, your Pixel 6 stops getting monthly security fixes from November 2026.

## The facts (checked 16 October 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| US launch | October 2021 |
| Google's promise | 5 years of OS and security updates from US launch |
| Guaranteed updates until | October 2026 |
| Latest update | October 2026 patch, build CP3A.261005.002.A1 (6 Oct 2026) |
| Pixel 6a | Different date: guaranteed until July 2027 |

Google's [Pixel update policy](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/4457705) puts the Pixel 6, 6 Pro and 6a in the group that gets "5 years of OS and security updates" from when each phone first went on sale in the US Google Store. The Pixel 6 and 6 Pro launched there in October 2021, so their window closes this month. The [October 2026 Pixel Update Bulletin](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/pixel/2026/2026-10-01) fixed several critical bugs, and [Droid Life's build list](https://www.droid-life.com/2026/10/06/2026-october-pixel-update-download/) confirms the Pixel 6 and 6 Pro were on it.

## What happens after October 2026?

- **No more monthly security patches.** The phone keeps working, but new Android vulnerabilities found from November on won't be fixed on it.
- **Google Play system updates continue for a while.** They patch some parts of Android, but only a small share of what a monthly update fixes.
- **Apps keep working for now.** Banking and payment apps tend to drop old patch levels eventually, not overnight.
- **The Pixel 6a is not affected yet.** It came out later and is guaranteed updates until July 2027.

## What should you do?

1. **Install the October 2026 update now:** Settings → System → Software update. Check that the security patch level reads 2026-10-05.
2. **Plan a replacement in the next few months** if it's your main phone, especially if you use it for banking, DigiD or work email.
3. **If you buy used or refurbished, pick a Pixel 8 or newer.** Google promises those 7 years of updates from their US launch, so a Pixel 8 is covered until October 2030 and newer models longer. A used Pixel 7 only has until October 2027, which is barely a year.
