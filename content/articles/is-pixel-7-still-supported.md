# Is the Pixel 7 still supported?

**Short answer: yes, for about one more year.** Google guarantees the Pixel 7 and Pixel 7 Pro updates **until October 2027**. Both phones got the **October 2026 security update** (patch level 2026-10-05) on 6 October 2026 and run **Android 17**. The **Pixel 7a** came out later, so its guarantee runs **until May 2028**. Your Pixel 7 is safe to use today, but it's in its last year of guaranteed updates.

## The facts (checked 16 October 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| US launch | October 2022 (Pixel 7 and 7 Pro), May 2023 (Pixel 7a) |
| Google's promise | 5 years of OS and security updates from US launch |
| Guaranteed updates until | October 2027 (Pixel 7 and 7 Pro), May 2028 (Pixel 7a) |
| Latest update | October 2026 patch, Android 17, build CP3A.261005.002.A1 (6 Oct 2026) |
| Pixel 8 and newer | 7 years of updates instead of 5 |

Google's [Pixel update policy](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/4457705) puts the Pixel 7, 7 Pro and 7a in the group that gets "5 years of OS and security updates" from when each phone first went on sale in the US Google Store. Google's [availability table](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15738422) lists October 2022 for the Pixel 7 and 7 Pro and May 2023 for the Pixel 7a. Add five years and you get October 2027 and May 2028.

The [October 2026 Pixel Update Bulletin](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/pixel/2026/2026-10-01) (published 6 October 2026) says all supported Google devices get the 2026-10-05 patch level. [Droid Life's build list](https://www.droid-life.com/2026/10/06/2026-october-pixel-update-download/) and Google's own [factory images page](https://developers.google.com/android/images) both list build CP3A.261005.002.A1 for the Pixel 7, 7 Pro and 7a, marked "17.0.0" (Android 17).

## What happens after October 2027?

- **No more monthly security patches.** The phone keeps working, but new Android vulnerabilities found after that won't be fixed on it.
- **Google Play system updates continue for a while.** They patch some parts of Android, but only a small share of what a monthly update fixes.
- **Apps keep working for now.** Banking and payment apps tend to drop old patch levels eventually, not overnight.
- **The Pixel 7a gets seven more months.** It's guaranteed updates until May 2028.

The Pixel 6 is a year ahead on the same path: its guarantee ends this month. Read [Is the Pixel 6 still supported?](../is-pixel-6-still-supported/) to see what that looks like.

## What should you do?

1. **Install the October 2026 update now:** Settings → System → Software update. Check that the security patch level reads 2026-10-05.
2. **Keep using it, but plan ahead.** A Pixel 7 is fine for banking, DigiD and work email until October 2027. Budget for a replacement some time in the next year.
3. **Don't buy a used Pixel 7 as your main phone in 2026.** It only has about a year of guaranteed updates left. **A Pixel 8 or newer is a better pick:** Google promises those 7 years of updates from their US launch. The Pixel 8 went on sale in the US in October 2023, so it's covered until October 2030, and newer models longer.
