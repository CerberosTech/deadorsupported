# When does the Galaxy S20 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: it already has.** Samsung took the **Galaxy S20, S20+ and S20 Ultra** off its security-update list in **April 2025**, after the **March 2025** patch. The **Galaxy S20 FE** (and S20 FE 5G) followed in **November 2025**, after the **October 2025** patch. None of the four models is on Samsung's update list today, and the last major version they'll ever get is **Android 13 with One UI 5.1**. The phones still work, but new security holes won't be fixed.

|---|---|---|

| Major Android upgrades | 3 (Android 11, 12, 13) | 3 (Android 11, 12, 13) |

Samsung's official [security update scope page](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) lists which phones still get monthly or quarterly patches. On 17 October 2026 the oldest Galaxy S phones on it are the **Galaxy S22** series and the **Galaxy S21 FE 5G** (both quarterly). No S20 model appears anywhere on the page.

## What the S20 got

- **Three major Android upgrades.** All S20 models launched with Android 10 and got Android 11, 12 and 13. Samsung's newsroom confirmed that **One UI 5.1** (Android 13) went to the whole S20 series, including the S20 FE and S20 FE 5G, starting in February 2023. There was no Android 14 / One UI 6 for any of them.
- **About five years of security patches.** The S20, S20+ and S20 Ultra moved from monthly to quarterly patches in April 2024 and dropped off the list in April 2025, five years after launch ([Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/samsung-galaxy/galaxy-s20-series-software-updates-samsung-support-ends), [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/samsung-stops-updates-for-galaxy-s20-a32-a52-a72/)). The S20 FE got its October 2025 patch, turned five that same month and was removed in November 2025 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/10/27/galaxy-s20-fe-security-updates-end/), [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/samsung-galaxy-s20-fe-software-updates-end/)).

## Could it still get an update?

Rarely, and don't count on it. In August 2025, months after support ended, Samsung pushed the **July 2025** security patch to the S20, S20+ and S20 Ultra **on Verizon in the US** ([Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/samsung-galaxy-s20-family-late-term-security-update/)). That was a one-off for one carrier, not a return to regular updates. SamMobile also notes that a final patch can still trickle out in markets that were behind. If you have an S20, check Settings → Software update → Download and install once, then assume that's it.

## What happens when updates stop?

- **New security holes stay open.** Each month Google and Samsung fix new flaws in Android. An S20 no longer gets those fixes, so the list of known, unpatched problems keeps growing.
- **Banking and payment apps are the main worry.** The phone is still fine for calls, photos and music. But for banking, payments, DigiD, work email or a child's main phone, an unpatched phone is a bigger risk every month.
- **Apps drift away over time.** Play Store app updates keep coming for a while, but apps eventually raise their minimum Android version. That happens app by app, not on a set date.

For a phone that's a year behind on the same path, read [Is the Galaxy S21 still supported?](../is-galaxy-s21-still-supported/) (regular updates ended in early 2026) or [Is the Galaxy S21 FE still supported?](../is-galaxy-s21-fe-still-supported/).

## What should you do?

1. **Install whatever update is offered, one last time:** Settings → Software update → Download and install.
2. **Move sensitive stuff off it.** Use a supported phone for banking, payments and work. Don't sideload apps on the S20.
3. **Keep it as a second device if you like.** A media player, camera, smart-home remote or offline music phone is a sensible retirement job.
4. **Don't buy one in 2026.** No S20 model gets regular updates any more, so a cheap one is only cheap because it's out of support.

Samsung doesn't print end dates on its security page, so treat those as estimates, not guarantees.
