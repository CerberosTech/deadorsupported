# When does the Galaxy S22 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: around February 2027.** The Galaxy S22, S22+ and S22 Ultra still get security updates as of October 2026, but only **quarterly**. Samsung's promise for this series was **four major Android upgrades and five years of security updates** from the February 2022 launch, which points to an end in early 2027. Samsung does not publish an exact end date on its security page.

## Where things stand (checked 16 October 2026)

| | Status |
|---|---|
| Launch | February 2022 (Android 12 / One UI 4.1) |
| Final major upgrade | Android 16 / One UI 8 (rolled out from late 2025) |
| Update frequency now | Quarterly security updates |
| Expected end of security updates | About February 2027 (five years after launch) |

Samsung's official [security update scope page](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) lists the "Galaxy S22, Galaxy S22+, Galaxy S22 Ultra" under **Current Models for Quarterly Security Updates**. Samsung adds that this list "is subject to change and will be reviewed on a periodic basis".

## The S22 got all four Android upgrades

The S22 shipped with Android 12 and went to Android 13, 14, 15 and finally **Android 16 with One UI 8**, the fourth and last major upgrade Samsung promised ([heise online](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Samsung-Galaxy-S22-receives-LineageOS-support-after-end-of-support-11416321.html)).

## Will it get One UI 8.5?

Probably not, and sources disagreed on this. When Samsung moved the S22 to quarterly updates in February 2026, [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s22-plus-ultra-have-lost-monthly-update-privileges/) expected one more One UI 8.5 release. [SammyGuru](https://sammyguru.com/galaxy-s22-june-2026-update-usa/) later reported that Samsung had stopped work on One UI 8.5 for the S22 and shipped only a June 2026 security patch. Samsung hasn't officially confirmed either way. Our view: assume **One UI 8 is the final version**.

Updates have also not followed a strict three-month rhythm. SammyGuru reports the S22 skipped the April 2026 patch, got May, and then got June unexpectedly. Timing varies by region and carrier.

## Where the "February 2027" date comes from

Samsung's launch-era policy for 2022 flagships was five years of security updates. Third-party trackers such as endoflife.date (as quoted by [MobileIQ](https://mobileiq.app/updates/galaxy-s22/)) record security support ending **25 February 2027**. heise also reports that Samsung says security patches continue until February 2027. Samsung's own security page doesn't print a date, so treat it as an expected date, not a guarantee to the day. Samsung sometimes releases a late patch for a critical flaw, but don't plan around that.

## What should S22 owners do?

- **Until early 2027:** keep installing updates (Settings → Software update). The phone is fine for banking and everyday use.
- **After support ends:** the phone keeps working, but new security holes won't be fixed. Consider replacing it for banking, work or a child's main phone, or use it as a backup/media device.
- **Tinkerers:** LineageOS now supports the S22 series ([heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Samsung-Galaxy-S22-receives-LineageOS-support-after-end-of-support-11416321.html)), which can extend its life. Flashing carries risk and may affect warranty and some apps.

## Buying a used S22 in 2026?

Only at a price that reflects roughly **four months of updates left**. A Galaxy S23 (on Samsung's *monthly* list) or a Galaxy S24 (up to seven years of updates) is a much safer long-term buy.
