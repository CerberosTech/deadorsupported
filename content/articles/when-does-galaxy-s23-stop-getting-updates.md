# When does the Galaxy S23 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: not yet, and not for a while.** On 17 October 2026 the **Galaxy S23, S23+, S23 Ultra and S23 FE** are all on Samsung's list for **monthly** security updates. Samsung promised **four generations of OS upgrades and five years of security updates** for every S23 model. The fourth and last upgrade, **One UI 9 with Android 17**, started rolling out to the S23, S23+ and S23 Ultra on **14 October 2026**. Samsung doesn't print end dates, so by our own maths (five years from launch) security updates should end around **February 2028** for the S23, S23+ and S23 Ultra and around **October 2028** for the S23 FE. Those two dates are **our estimate**, not Samsung's.

## The facts (checked 17 October 2026)

| | S23, S23+, S23 Ultra | S23 FE |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Announced 1 February 2023, in stores from 17 February 2023, with Android 13 / One UI 5.1 | Announced 4 October 2023, on sale from 5 October 2023, with Android 13 |
| Samsung's promise | 4 generations of OS upgrades, 5 years of security updates | 4 generations of OS upgrades, 5 years of security updates |
| Latest major version | One UI 9 / Android 17 (rolling out from 14 Oct 2026, South Korea first) | One UI 8.5 / Android 16 (from June 2026); One UI 9 promised "later this year" |
| Update frequency now | Monthly | Monthly |
| Moves to quarterly | Not announced (our estimate: early 2027) | Not announced |
| Security updates end | Not announced (our estimate: about February 2028) | Not announced (our estimate: about October 2028) |

Samsung's official [security update scope page](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) lists "Galaxy S23, Galaxy S23+, Galaxy S23 Ultra, Galaxy S23 FE" under **Current Models for Monthly Security Updates**. Samsung adds that the monthly list "is subject to change as the regular support period expires", and that some carriers only ship quarterly updates even for phones on the monthly list.

## Is One UI 9 the S23's last major update?

Yes, as far as Samsung's promise goes. The S23 launched on Android 13 and has had **Android 14 (One UI 6), Android 15 (One UI 7) and Android 16 (One UI 8)**, plus the One UI 8.5 point release that reached the S23 series globally from **21 May 2026** ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s23-one-ui-8-5-stable-update-released-globally/)). **Android 17 with One UI 9** is the fourth generation, which completes the promise. Stable One UI 9 started reaching the S23, S23+ and S23 Ultra in **South Korea on 14 October 2026**, beta testers first, with the **October 2026** security patch ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s23-stable-one-ui-9-0-android-17-update-out/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/15/samsung-just-released-android-17-updates-for-galaxy-s23-and-several-more-devices/), [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/one-ui-9-galaxy-s23-3723034/)). Samsung hasn't published a schedule for other countries or US carriers yet. Based on earlier rollouts, press reports expect other regions within a week or two.

Samsung hasn't said whether a later **One UI 9.x** point release will reach the S23. Don't count on it: the Galaxy S22 got its fourth upgrade (One UI 8) but, according to reports, no One UI 8.5.

## What about the Galaxy S23 FE?

The S23 FE has the same promise (four OS upgrades, five years of security updates, per [Samsung's launch announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-galaxy-s23-fe-galaxy-tab-s9-fe-and-galaxy-buds-fe-bring-standout-features-to-even-more-users)) and is also on the **monthly** list. It wasn't part of the 14 October One UI 9 rollout. Samsung Thailand first listed the S23 FE for October, then changed that to "later this year" ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/samsung-may-have-delayed-one-ui-9-0-release-galaxy-s23-fe/), [SammyGuru](https://sammyguru.com/one-ui-9-rollout-tracker/)). Because it launched eight months after the regular S23, its five years of security updates should last until about **October 2028** (our estimate).

## When will the S23 move to quarterly updates?

Samsung hasn't said. Our estimate is **early 2027**, because that's what happened to the generation before: Samsung moved the Galaxy S22 series from monthly to quarterly updates in **February 2026**, four years after its launch ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s22-plus-ultra-have-lost-monthly-update-privileges/)). Quarterly patches are still security updates, just less often. Samsung can also move phones earlier or later, so check the [official list](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) rather than relying on any date.

## Where our end-of-support estimates come from

- **Samsung's promise:** "four generations of OS upgrades and five years of security updates" for the S23 series, in its [February 2023 launch announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/take-your-passions-further-with-the-new-samsung-galaxy-s23-series-designed-for-a-premium-experience-today-and-beyond) and on its [sustainability page](https://www.samsung.com/global/sustainability/popup/popup_doc/AYUposjaA_oAIx_C/) ("for all Galaxy S23 series models").
- **The maths:** five years from February 2023 is **February 2028**; five years from October 2023 is **October 2028**. Support is tied to the model's launch, not to when you bought your phone.
- **The track record:** the [Galaxy S20](../when-does-galaxy-s20-stop-getting-updates/) dropped off Samsung's list about five years after launch, and the [Galaxy S22](../when-does-galaxy-s22-stop-getting-updates/) is on quarterly updates with an expected end around February 2027.

Timing still varies by country and carrier, and a final patch can arrive a little after the five-year mark. Treat these as planning dates, not guarantees to the day.

## Is a Galaxy S23 still worth buying used in 2026?

It can be, at the right price. A used S23 bought in October 2026 should get roughly **another 15 months of monthly or quarterly security updates** (our estimate, to about February 2028), and it's getting its last major Android version now. That's fine for a budget phone or a phone for a teenager, but it's **less than half** of what a Galaxy S24 or Pixel 8 still has left. If you buy one, make sure it's on One UI 8.5 or later and check for updates straight away: Settings → Software update → Download and install.

## What should S23 owners do?

1. **Install updates as they arrive.** Settings → Software update → Download and install. If you're outside South Korea, One UI 9 should appear in the coming weeks.
2. **Keep using it with confidence until 2027.** It's fully supported today, including for banking, payments, DigiD and work email.
3. **Plan a replacement for early 2028.** After security updates stop, the phone keeps working, but new security holes won't be fixed.
