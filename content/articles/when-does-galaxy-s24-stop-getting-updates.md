# When does the Galaxy S24 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: not for years.** On 18 October 2026 the **Galaxy S24, S24+, S24 Ultra and S24 FE** are all on Samsung's list for **monthly** security updates, and they're getting **One UI 9 with Android 17** right now. Samsung promised **seven generations of OS upgrades and seven years of security updates** for the S24 series, and One UI 9 is only the **third** of those seven upgrades. Samsung doesn't print end dates, so by our own maths security updates should run until about **early 2031** for the S24, S24+ and S24 Ultra and about **autumn 2031** for the S24 FE. Those dates are **our estimate**, not Samsung's.

## The facts (checked 18 October 2026)

| | S24, S24+, S24 Ultra | S24 FE |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | Announced 17 January 2024; Samsung announced worldwide availability on 30 January 2024; shipped with Android 14 / One UI 6.1 | Announced 26 September 2024, shipped with Android 14 |
| Samsung's promise | 7 generations of OS upgrades, 7 years of security updates | 7 generations of OS upgrades, 7 years of security updates |
| Latest major version | One UI 9 / Android 17 (South Korea from 6-7 Oct 2026, Europe and India from 12 Oct 2026) | One UI 9 / Android 17 (South Korea first, then more regions; Verizon in the US from 13 Oct 2026) |
| OS upgrades used so far | 3 of 7 (Android 15, 16 and 17) | 3 of 7 |
| Update frequency now | Monthly | Monthly |
| Security updates end | Not announced (our estimate: about January 2031) | Not announced (our estimate: about autumn 2031) |

Samsung's official [security update scope page](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) lists "Galaxy S24, Galaxy S24+, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Galaxy S24 FE" under **Current Models for Monthly Security Updates**. Samsung adds that some carriers only ship quarterly updates, even for phones on the monthly list.

## Is the Galaxy S24 getting One UI 9?

Yes, it's rolling out now. Stable One UI 9 started reaching the S24, S24+ and S24 Ultra in **South Korea on 6 to 7 October 2026** ([GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_rolls_out_stable_one_ui_9_based_on_android_17_to_the_galaxy_s24_series_galaxy_z_fold6_and_fl-news-74932.php), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/06/samsung-android-17-galaxy-s24-fold-6-rolling-out/)) and widened to **Europe and India on 12 October** ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s24-stable-one-ui-9-0-update-rollout-begins/)). In the US, carrier models on AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon got it from 12 October, with unlocked phones following later ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s24-stable-one-ui-9-0-update-reaches-usa/), [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/samsung-one-ui-9-global-updates-galaxy-s24-z-fold-6-z-flip-6-galaxy-a57-first-big-bug_id184028)). The **S24 FE** got it on Verizon on 13 October ([Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/samsung-galaxy-s24-fe-update/)).

Coming from One UI 8.5, the download is about **3.8 GB** ([GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_rolls_out_stable_one_ui_9_based_on_android_17_to_the_galaxy_s24_series_galaxy_z_fold6_and_fl-news-74932.php)). **Back up first:** PhoneArena reported on 13 October that some owners of several Samsung models, including the S24 and S24 Ultra, got stuck on boot errors after updating, and described a Safe Mode workaround ([PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/samsung-one-ui-9-global-updates-galaxy-s24-z-fold-6-z-flip-6-galaxy-a57-first-big-bug_id184028)).

## How many Android upgrades are left?

Four, if Samsung keeps its promise. The S24 launched on Android 14 and has had **Android 15 (One UI 7), Android 16 (One UI 8)** and now **Android 17 (One UI 9)**. Seven generations means four more major upgrades after this one. If Google keeps its yearly rhythm, the last would be Android 21 around 2030. That version number is **our maths**, not a Samsung statement.

## Where our end-of-support estimates come from

- **Samsung's promise:** "seven generations of OS upgrades and seven years of security updates", in its [S24 launch announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/enter-the-new-era-of-mobile-ai-with-samsung-galaxy-s24-series) and its [availability announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-galaxy-s24-series-is-now-available-worldwide) of 30 January 2024. The S24 FE got the same promise in its [September 2024 announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/galaxy-s24-series-expands-with-s24-fe-a-premium-experience-that-makes-full-galaxy-ai-capabilities-attainable-for-more-users).
- **The maths:** seven years from January 2024 is **January 2031**; seven years from September to October 2024 is **autumn 2031**. Support is tied to the model's launch, not to when you bought your phone.
- **The track record:** Samsung has kept its earlier promises. The [Galaxy S20](../when-does-galaxy-s20-stop-getting-updates/) dropped off the list about five years after launch, and the [Galaxy S22](../when-does-galaxy-s22-stop-getting-updates/) moved to quarterly updates after four years. Expect the S24 to slow to **quarterly** updates some years before the end, like older models did; Samsung hasn't said when.

Timing still varies by country and carrier. Treat these as planning dates, not guarantees to the day.

## Is a Galaxy S24 worth buying used or refurbished?

**Yes, it's one of the best used Samsung buys right now.** A used S24 bought in October 2026 should get roughly **four more years** of security updates (our estimate, to about January 2031) and four more Android upgrades. Compare that with the [Galaxy S23](../when-does-galaxy-s23-stop-getting-updates/), which is getting its **last** major upgrade now and has security updates until about February 2028 (our estimate). If you buy one, check it's on One UI 9 or at least One UI 8.5 and install updates straight away: Settings → Software update → Download and install.

## What should S24 owners do?

1. **Install One UI 9 when it appears,** after a backup. Settings → Software update → Download and install.
2. **Don't think about replacing it for years.** It's fully supported, including for banking, payments, DigiD and work email.
3. **Replace the battery, not the phone,** if it gets weak. With updates until about 2031, a new battery is usually the cheaper fix.
