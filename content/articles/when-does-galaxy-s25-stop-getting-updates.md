# When does the Galaxy S25 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: not until about 2032.** On 18 October 2026 the **Galaxy S25, S25+, S25 Ultra, S25 Edge and S25 FE** are all on Samsung's list for **monthly** security updates, and they're getting **One UI 9 with Android 17** now. Samsung promises **seven generations of OS upgrades and seven years of security updates "from the global launch date"**. Samsung doesn't print end dates, so by our own maths security updates should run until about **early 2032** for the S25, S25+ and S25 Ultra, about **May 2032** for the S25 Edge and about **September 2032** for the S25 FE. Those dates are **our estimate**, not Samsung's.

Samsung's official [security update scope page](https://security.samsungmobile.com/workScope.smsb) lists "Galaxy S25, Galaxy S25+, Galaxy S25 Ultra, Galaxy S25 Edge, Galaxy S25 FE" under **Current Models for Monthly Security Updates**. Samsung adds that some carriers only ship quarterly updates, even for phones on the monthly list.

## Is the Galaxy S25 getting One UI 9?

Yes, it's rolling out now. Samsung ended the beta and released stable One UI 9 for the **S25, S25+ and S25 Ultra** in **South Korea on 28 September 2026**, and for the **S25 Edge and S25 FE** a few hours later the same day ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s25-edge-s25-fe-get-stable-one-ui-9-0-update/)). US carrier models followed from 3 October and unlocked phones from 5 October ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jaymcgregor/2026/10/08/samsung-galaxy-s25-android-17-update--one-ui-9-us-release-z-fold-7/), [Samsung Magazine](https://www.samsungmagazine.eu/en/2026/10/06/galaxy-s25-one-ui-9/)). In **Europe** the S25, S25+ and S25 Ultra got it on **6 October**, firmware S93xBXXUCDZIF, with a download of over 3 GB if you weren't in the beta ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s25-stable-one-ui-9-update-released-globally/)).

For the **S25 Edge and S25 FE in Europe**, reports differ: Dutch site Galaxyclub listed European firmware for both on 6 October, and an S25 FE owner there said it was arriving ([Galaxyclub](https://www.galaxyclub.nl/nieuws/galaxy-s25-update-android-17-one-ui-9/)), while other trackers hadn't confirmed it yet. If you don't see it, it's on its way: check Settings → Software update → Download and install.

## Is the S25 still getting monthly security patches?

Yes. All five models are on Samsung's monthly list. When One UI 9 arrived in Europe it came with the **September 2026** security patch, not October's ([Galaxyclub](https://www.galaxyclub.nl/nieuws/galaxy-s25-update-android-17-one-ui-9/)). Samsung published its **October 2026** security bulletin with fixes for nine critical Android flaws ([Samsung Mobile Security](https://security.samsungmobile.com/securityUpdate.smsb)); expect it on the S25 as a separate update after One UI 9, timing by region and carrier. To check your level: Settings → About phone → Software information → Android security patch level.

## How many Android upgrades are left?

- **S25, S25+, S25 Ultra and S25 Edge:** five. They launched on Android 15 and have had **Android 16 (One UI 8)** and now **Android 17 (One UI 9)**. Seven generations means five more after this one; on Google's yearly rhythm the last would be **Android 22**.
- **S25 FE:** six. It launched on Android 16, so One UI 9 is its **first** of seven upgrades; the last would be **Android 23**.

Those version numbers are **our maths**, not a Samsung statement. Samsung's own wording is "seven generations of OS upgrades".

## Where our end-of-support estimates come from

- **Samsung's promise:** "seven generations of OS upgrades and seven years of security updates", in its [S25 launch announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-galaxy-s25-series-sets-the-standard-of-ai-phone-as-a-true-ai-companion). Samsung's UAE newsroom adds that the S25 series is supported "from the global launch date" ([Samsung Gulf](https://www.samsung.com/ae/news/local/samsung-announces-pre-orders-for-galaxy-s25-series-in-the-uae/)), and the [S25 FE announcement](https://news.samsung.com/global/meet-samsung-galaxy-s25-fe-the-gateway-to-the-galaxy-ai-and-flagship-essentials) uses the same words. SamMobile confirmed the same promise for the S25 Edge at its launch ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s25-edge-seven-years-android-one-ui-security-updates/)).
- **The maths:** seven years from the January/February 2025 launch is **about February 2032**; from May 2025 (Edge) **about May 2032**; from September 2025 (FE) **about September 2032**. Support is tied to the model's launch, not to when you bought your phone.
- **The track record:** Samsung has kept its earlier promises. The [Galaxy S20](../when-does-galaxy-s20-stop-getting-updates/) dropped off the list about five years after launch, and the [Galaxy S22](../when-does-galaxy-s22-stop-getting-updates/) moved to quarterly updates after four years. Expect the S25 to slow to **quarterly** updates some years before the end, like older models did; Samsung hasn't said when.

Timing still varies by country and carrier. Treat these as planning dates, not guarantees to the day.

## Is a Galaxy S25 worth buying used or refurbished?

**Yes, if the price is right.** A used S25 bought in October 2026 should get roughly **five more years** of security updates (our estimate, to about February 2032) and five more Android upgrades. A used [Galaxy S24](../when-does-galaxy-s24-stop-getting-updates/) gets about a year less (to about January 2031, our estimate) and is usually cheaper, so compare the price per year left. The **S25 FE** has the longest runway of the five (about September 2032, our estimate) but it's the cheaper, lower-spec model. If you buy one, check it's on One UI 9 or at least One UI 8.5 and install updates straight away.

## What should S25 owners do?

1. **Install One UI 9 when it appears,** after a backup. Settings → Software update → Download and install.
2. **Then install the October patch** when it shows up as a separate update.
3. **Don't think about replacing it for software reasons.** It's fully supported, including for banking, payments, DigiD and work email, for years.
4. **Replace the battery, not the phone,** if it gets weak later on. With updates until about 2032, a new battery is usually the cheaper fix.
