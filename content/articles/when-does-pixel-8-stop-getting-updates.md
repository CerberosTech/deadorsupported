# When does the Pixel 8 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: October 2030** for the **Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro**, and **May 2031** for the **Pixel 8a**. Those aren't guesses: Google promises **7 years of Android version and security updates** from the month each phone first went on sale in the US Google Store, and it prints those exact months on its own support pages. On 18 October 2026 all three phones run **Android 17** and got the **October 2026 security update**, so they get exactly the same updates as the newest Pixels.

## The facts (checked 18 October 2026)

| | Pixel 8 and 8 Pro | Pixel 8a |
|---|---|---|
| On sale in the US Google Store | October 2023 (shipped with Android 14) | May 2024 (shipped with Android 14) |
| Google's promise | 7 years of OS and security updates | 7 years of OS and security updates |
| Guaranteed updates until at least | **October 2030** | **May 2031** |
| Current Android version | Android 17 (QPR1, September 2026 Pixel Drop) | Android 17 (QPR1, September 2026 Pixel Drop) |
| Latest update | October 2026, build CP3A.261005.005 (from 6 Oct 2026) | October 2026, build CP3A.261005.005 |
| Years of updates left | About 4 | About 4.5 |

Google's [Pixel update page](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/4457705) says "Pixel 8 and later phones will get updates for 7 years starting from when the device first became available on the Google Store in the US", and its [availability table](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15738422) lists **October 2023** for the Pixel 8 and 8 Pro and **May 2024** for the Pixel 8a. Google's older version of the same help page spelled it out as "guaranteed security updates until at least" **October 2030** and **May 2031**.

## Is the Pixel 8 still getting updates right now?

Yes. Google started rolling out the **October 2026 update** on **6 October 2026** to "all supported Pixel devices running Android 17", and the Pixel 8, 8 Pro and 8a are on the build list with **CP3A.261005.005** ([Google Pixel Community](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/thread/471669543/google-pixel-update-october-2026?hl=en)). The update fixes the on-screen keyboard sometimes not appearing in search fields and ringtone distortion in some internet (VoIP) calls on the Pixel 8 series and newer. The [Pixel Security Bulletin](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/pixel) has the security fixes; Talk Android counted three critical ones, including a Bluetooth flaw ([Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/532944-october-pixel-security-update/)).

The phones also got **Android 17 QPR1** in the **September 2026 Pixel Drop**, which went to every Pixel from the Pixel 6 up ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/android-17-qpr1-update-september-pixel-drop-supported-phones-3711582/)).

To check yours: **Settings → System → Software updates → System update**.

## How many Android upgrades are left?

The Pixel 8 launched with Android 14 and has had **Android 15, 16 and 17**, so it's three big upgrades in. Google's promise covers OS updates until October 2030, so on Google's usual yearly rhythm that's roughly **four more major versions**. That count is **our maths**, not a Google statement; the October 2030 date is Google's.

## What happens after October 2030?

- **No more monthly security patches** from Google. The phone keeps working, but new holes don't get fixed.
- **Google Play system updates keep coming for a while.** They patch some parts of Android, but far less than a monthly update.
- **Apps follow later.** Banking, payment and work apps usually stop supporting old Android versions gradually, not on day one.

For comparison, the [Pixel 6](../is-pixel-6-still-supported/) is at that point right now: its guaranteed updates ran until October 2026. The [Pixel 7](../is-pixel-7-still-supported/) follows in October 2027.

## Is a Pixel 8 worth buying used or refurbished in 2026?

**Yes, as long as the price reflects that it's the oldest 7-year Pixel.** A used Pixel 8 bought now still has about **four years** of guaranteed updates, which beats a used Pixel 7 (about one year) by a mile. If you can find a **Pixel 8a** for a similar price, it's covered seven months longer (May 2031). A **Pixel 9** is covered until August 2031, per the same [Google table](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15738422).

Before you pay, check the phone is on **Android 17** and recent patches, that it isn't carrier-locked, and that the previous owner removed their Google account (otherwise Factory Reset Protection locks you out).

## What should Pixel 8 owners do?

1. **Install the October 2026 update** if you haven't: Settings → System → Software updates.
2. **Don't replace it for software reasons.** It's fully supported until October 2030, including for banking, payments, DigiD and work email.
3. **If the battery is weak, replace the battery, not the phone.** With four years of updates left, that's usually the cheaper fix.
