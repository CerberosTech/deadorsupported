# When does the Pixel 9 stop getting updates?

**Short answer: not before August 2031** for the **Pixel 9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL and 9 Pro Fold**, and **April 2032** for the **Pixel 9a**. Google promises **7 years of Android version and security updates** from the month each phone first went on sale in the US Google Store, and publishes those months itself. On 18 October 2026 all five phones run **Android 17** and got the **October 2026 update**, the same build as the Pixel 8 and Pixel 10.

## The facts (checked 18 October 2026)

| | Pixel 9, 9 Pro XL | Pixel 9 Pro, 9 Pro Fold | Pixel 9a |
|---|---|---|---|
| On sale in the US Google Store (Google's table) | August 2024 (shipped with Android 14) | September 2024 (shipped with Android 14) | April 2025 (shipped with Android 15) |
| Google's promise | 7 years of OS and security updates | 7 years of OS and security updates | 7 years of OS and security updates |
| Guaranteed updates until at least | **August 2031** | **August 2031** (see note below) | **April 2032** |
| Current Android version | Android 17 (QPR1, September 2026 Pixel Drop) | Android 17 (QPR1) | Android 17 (QPR1) |
| Latest update | October 2026, build CP3A.261005.005 (from 6 Oct 2026) | October 2026, build CP3A.261005.005 | October 2026, build CP3A.261005.005 |
| Android upgrades so far | 3 (Android 15, 16, 17) | 3 (Android 15, 16, 17) | 2 (Android 16, 17) |
| Years of updates left | About 4.8 | About 4.8 or more | About 5.5 |

Google's [Pixel update page](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/4457705) says "Pixel 8 and later phones will get updates for 7 years starting from when the device first became available on the Google Store in the US", and lists all five Pixel 9 models. Its [availability table](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15738422) gives **August 2024** for the Pixel 9 and 9 Pro XL, **September 2024** for the Pixel 9 Pro and 9 Pro Fold, and **April 2025** for the Pixel 9a.

**A note on the 9 Pro and 9 Pro Fold:** before December 2024, Google's own help page printed "guaranteed until at least" dates, and it listed **August 2031** for every Pixel 9 model, including the 9 Pro and 9 Pro Fold ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/04/16/google-slightly-less-explicit-with-pixel-end-of-life-dates/)). Google's current table now says those two went on sale in September 2024, which by its own rule would mean September 2031. We use **August 2031**, the earlier date Google published, so you're not planning on a month that might not come.

## Is the Pixel 9 still getting updates right now?

Yes. Google started rolling out the **October 2026 update** on **6 October 2026** to "all supported Pixel devices running Android 17", and every Pixel 9 model is on the list with build **CP3A.261005.005** ([Google Pixel Community](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/thread/471669543/google-pixel-update-october-2026?hl=en)). For the Pixel 9 series it fixes the on-screen keyboard sometimes not appearing in search fields and ringtone distortion in some internet (VoIP) calls. The security fixes are in the [Pixel Security Bulletin](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/pixel).

**Android 17** reached the Pixel 9 series on launch day, **16 June 2026** ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/16/google-android-17-pixel-launch/)), and **Android 17 QPR1** followed in the **September 2026 Pixel Drop**, which went to every Pixel from the Pixel 6 up ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/android-17-qpr1-update-september-pixel-drop-supported-phones-3711582/)).

To check yours: **Settings → System → Software updates → System update**.

## How many Android upgrades are left?

The Pixel 9, 9 Pro, 9 Pro XL and 9 Pro Fold launched with **Android 14** ([Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/pixel-9-series-wont-ship-android-15/)) and have had Android 15, 16 and 17. The Pixel 9a launched with Android 15 and has had 16 and 17. With Google's guarantee running to August 2031 (April 2032 for the 9a) and Google releasing a big version every year, that's roughly **five more major versions** for all of them. That count is **our maths**, not a Google statement; the end months are Google's.

## What happens after August 2031?

- **No more monthly security patches** from Google. The phone keeps working, but new holes don't get fixed.
- **Google Play system updates keep coming for a while.** They patch some parts of Android, but far less than a monthly update.
- **Apps follow later.** Banking, payment and work apps usually stop supporting old Android versions gradually, not on day one.

The [Pixel 6](../is-pixel-6-still-supported/) is at that point right now: its guaranteed updates ran until October 2026.

## Is a Pixel 9 worth buying used or refurbished in 2026?

**Yes, it's one of the safest used Android buys right now.** A used Pixel 9 bought today still has almost **five years** of guaranteed updates, about ten months more than a [Pixel 8](../when-does-pixel-8-stop-getting-updates/) (October 2030). The **Pixel 9a** is usually the cheapest of the five and is covered the longest, to **April 2032**. The 9 Pro Fold is the expensive outlier: same update window, but a folding screen is costlier to repair.

Before you pay, check the phone is on **Android 17** with a recent patch, that it isn't carrier-locked, and that the previous owner removed their Google account (otherwise Factory Reset Protection locks you out). Comparing with other brands? See [which used or refurbished phone to buy in 2026](../best-used-phone-to-buy-2026/), ranked by years of updates left.

## What should Pixel 9 owners do?

1. **Install the October 2026 update** if you haven't: Settings → System → Software updates.
2. **Don't replace it for software reasons.** It's fully supported until at least August 2031, including for banking, payments, DigiD and work email.
3. **If the battery gets weak, replace the battery, not the phone.** With about five years of updates left, that's usually the cheaper fix.
